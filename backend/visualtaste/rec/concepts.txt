# scored against the preference vector with the SigLIP 2 text encoder, and
# reported as z-scores against the mean concept similarity -- the raw cosines
# are useless on their own because of the image-text modality gap.

# and "overcast" are useful feedback; "high aesthetic quality" is not, and
# neither is anything so broad it matches everything.
